# The Fantasy League

An NFL fantasy football league running since 2012 (10–12 teams to 2014, 16 since 2015): on NFL.com (as TheGentlemen'sLeague) from 2012 to 2024, on Sleeper from 2025. This site is its record book and one-stop shop.

## Language

### Identity

**Franchise**:
A continuous run in one league slot under the same management, as the league judges it; the core entity every record hangs off. Run by one or more managers, none of them the lead.
_Avoid_: Team, roster, slot

**Franchise name**:
The name a franchise is shown by: the latest team name it played under, on Sleeper or NFL.com. The hand-kept tables may also use the name the franchise is filed under, the last team name it played under on NFL.com, which a rename on Sleeper doesn't change.
_Avoid_: Team name (for the franchise as a whole)

**Manager**:
The person who runs a franchise. One manager may run several franchises over time; real names are never published.
_Avoid_: Owner, user, player

**Commissioner**:
The manager with the final say on the league's rulings. The role belongs to the person, not their franchise, and can be handed over on its own.
_Avoid_: Commish, admin

**Active franchise**:
A franchise playing in the current season. Departed franchises keep every record they set.
_Avoid_: Current team

**Team name**:
The name a franchise played under in a given season. Every franchise has one for each season it plays, even if it's only a placeholder name. It changes most years and never identifies anything.
_Avoid_: Franchise

**Placeholder name**:
A name a platform shows when a franchise has no real team name, such as "pending". It counts as that season's team name but never becomes a franchise name.

**NFL player**:
A real-world NFL athlete rostered, drafted, kept or traded by franchises. Known from 2017 for drafts, 2021 for keepers, and the Sleeper era for everything else.
_Avoid_: Player (unqualified)

### Rosters

**Lineup**:
A franchise's starters and bench in a week it played a game, with each NFL player's points.
_Avoid_: Roster (for a single week)

**Transaction**:
A completed roster move: a trade, a won waiver claim or a free-agent pickup.
_Avoid_: Move, add/drop

**Waiver claim**:
A franchise's bid for an NFL player in one waiver run, won or lost.
_Avoid_: Waiver (unqualified), bid

**Draft pick**:
A slot in a season's draft, belonging to one franchise and tradeable, so it may be used by another.

**Keeper**:
An NFL player a franchise actually keeps from one season into the next instead of releasing them into the draft, at the cost of a draft round. Being eligible to keep a player doesn't make them a keeper. The league stopped keeping players from 2026.

### Structure

**Season**:
One year of the league, from draft to the end of the brackets.
_Avoid_: Year, league (for a single year)

**Current season**:
The season being played or, between seasons, the one that finished most recently, until the next season's week 1. "This season" always means the current season.
_Avoid_: Live season, latest season

**Format**:
How a season is played: team count, divisions, regular-season length, and each bracket's size, seeding, tiebreaks and weeks. It changes between seasons.
_Avoid_: Rules, settings

**Era**:
The platform a season was played on: the NFL.com era (2012–2024) or the Sleeper era (2025 on).

**Division**:
A group of franchises in one season whose standings drive bracket seeding: one division to 2014, four groups of four since 2015.
_Avoid_: Group, conference

**Realignment**:
The league redrawing its divisions, currently every three years.

**Division lineage**:
The same division across the seasons between two realignments, even if one of its franchises changes hands.

### Games and brackets

**Game**:
A head-to-head pairing of two franchises in one week that counts in the league's results: regular season or bracket. Pairings the platform invented to fill a schedule are not games.
_Avoid_: Matchup, fixture, exhibition

**Season line**:
A franchise's season summed up: W-L-T, points for and against, division, seed, bracket and final placing.
_Avoid_: Standings row, record (for a whole season)

**Tie**:
A game whose two franchises scored the same points. Records are written W-L-T.
_Avoid_: Draw

**Bracket**:
One of the postseason competitions a season ends in. Each is defined by its role; its size and seeding change between seasons.
_Avoid_: Postseason, playoffs (for all brackets)

**Playoffs**:
The bracket that decides the champion.
_Avoid_: Winners bracket, championship bracket

**Shit Bowl**:
The bracket that decides the worst franchise of the season. Losing advances you.
_Avoid_: Toilet bowl, losers bracket, consolation bracket

**Shit Bowl winner**:
The franchise the Shit Bowl crowns worst of the season.
_Avoid_: Shit Bowl loser, last place

**Playoff picture**:
The current season's franchises ordered by the seed each would get if the regular season ended now, with the cut-offs between brackets. Franchises still level after every tiebreak except scissors-paper-rock share a seed.
_Avoid_: Projected standings, live standings

**Clinch**:
A bracket place or seed range a franchise is guaranteed, whatever happens in the remaining regular-season games. Its opposite is elimination: a bracket place a franchise can no longer reach.
_Avoid_: Lock, magic number

**Mediocre Bowl**:
The bracket for franchises in neither the Playoffs nor the Shit Bowl. Only exists since 2015; from 2026 it is a best-of-three series between the two middle franchises.

**Series**:
A bracket round in which the same two franchises meet over several weeks.

**Roll of honour**:
Each season's champion, runner-up, Shit Bowl winner and Mediocre Bowl winner, one row per season.
_Avoid_: Honours board, history

**Ruling**:
A decision the league makes rather than one the data records: a franchise identity call, a scissors-paper-rock result, a settled dispute, or a season's format. The record book only records rulings and never makes them.
_Avoid_: Decision, correction

**Disputed**:
A hand-recorded outcome the league can't agree on, with more than one candidate. It counts for nobody until it's settled.

### Measures

**Breakdown**:
A franchise's W-L-T record had it played every other franchise each regular-season week. Used as a tiebreaker.
_Avoid_: All-play record

**Award**:
A named per-season title with a fixed formula, given to the franchise that ranks first on it (tied franchises are co-winners) once the regular season ends. Light-hearted: the champion, the Shit Bowl winner and Breakdown are not awards.
_Avoid_: Trophy, prize

**Award leader**:
The franchise ranking first on an award while the regular season is still being played.

**Award result**:
One franchise's value and rank for one award in one season.

**Luck**:
A franchise's win % in a season minus its Breakdown %, a tie counting as half a win. Positive is lucky.

**Record**:
A ranked list of franchises, seasons, games or divisions on one measure, such as highest score or most championships. The record book is made of them.
_Avoid_: Leaderboard, record (for a W-L-T line)
