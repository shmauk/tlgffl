### 2.2 Derivation logic in `build.py`

**Franchise identity** (`build.py:58-78`):
- key = `(teamId, ownerUserId)` per NFL season, so a franchise is one manager's continuous run in one slot.
- `SAME_MANAGER` (`build.py:41-44`) collapses two account changes: seat 2 moved NFL accounts for 2016, and seat 3's co-managers swapped primary in 2015. Every other `ownerUserId` change is treated as a real handover. `coManagerUserId` is otherwise ignored.
- Display name = the most recent team name that isn't `pending`. That can come from the 2025 phantom NFL season.
- Sleeper era: roster → franchise via `sleeper_aliases.csv` only. So cross-era continuity is a human judgement, not data.
- Result: **20 franchises**, 16 active. One is Sleeper-only (new in 2025). Four have left (spans 2012–13, 2012–22, 2012–24, 2015–21).

**Regular-season length** (`build.py:96-116`): weeks = max(W+L+T) per season from the API, giving 14 (2012–14), 13 (2015–20) and 14 (2021–24). For Sleeper the phase is `week < playoff_week_start` (`build.py:134`).

**Games** (`build.py:119-148`): NFL games are tagged regular or playoff by week. Sleeper games are paired by `matchup_id`. Rows with a null `matchup_id` are dropped, and so are 0–0 pairs (unplayed weeks).

**Season standings** (`build.py:153-226`): NFL comes from API season stats. Fields: `record`, W/L/T, PF/PA, `seasonRank`, `finish` (=`place`), `playoffSeed`, `madePlayoffs` (bracket type = championship), `finalOrder`, `adds`, `trades`. Sleeper comes from roster settings, with `finish` taken from winners-bracket placement games (`p` → winner gets p, loser p+1). `adds`/`trades` are counted from completed transactions; trades are counted once per roster involved and halved for league totals (`build.py:575-576`).

**Playoff bracket reconstruction** (`build.py:231-269`): NFL games are rebuilt from seeds plus final places, using a re-seeding rule (top seed draws the lowest surviving seed). This is validated against the game log for 2012–2020 (46/46 pairings). It handles 4-team (2012–14) and 6-team (2015+) brackets. Reconstructed games carry a winner and get scores only where the scrape logged that game (`bracketScored` = 2012–2021, 2025). Playoff records come from the brackets, not the log. Consolation games count towards **neither** phase (`build.py:437-440`, meta note).

**Finals / podium** (`build.py:336-373`): champion, runner-up and third come from `finalStandingsTeamIds` (NFL) or bracket placements (Sleeper). The 2025 NFL phantom is excluded.

**Shit Bowl** (`build.py:294-334, 584-596`): CSV for 2012–2024. From 2025 the winner of the Sleeper **losers bracket** `p == 1` game is "crowned worst", counted only once that game has been played. There is a loud failure if a name doesn't match a franchise.

**Head-to-head and records aggregation** (`build.py:394-559`):
- Per franchise: `allTime.{regular,playoff,combined}` with `wins`, `losses`, `draws`, `pointsFor`, `pointsAgainst`, `scoredGames`, `gamesPlayed`, `record`, `winPercentage` (=(W+0.5D)/G, rounded to 0.1), `avgPointsFor`, `avgPointsAgainst` (divided by `scoredGames`, not games).
- **Regular-season career totals come from the platforms' season records (all seasons)**, not from the game log. Head-to-head (`opponentHistory`) comes only from logged games, so it has no 2022 wk10–2024 regular-season meetings. Playoff head-to-head covers every season.
- Also: `titles`, `playoffAppearances`, `finalsWon`/`Lost`/`Reached`, `shitbowls`, `shitbowlsDisputed`, `adds`, `trades`, `bestWeek`/`worstWeek` (from regular-season logged weeks only), `seasons`, `firstSeason`, `lastSeason`, `active` (in the latest Sleeper season or on a 2026 roster).

**Corrections already made** (README "What the rebuild fixed"): win % was truncated, not rounded (16 of 282 H2H entries); five in-progress 2022 wk10 games had been filed as results; ties had been scored as away wins.

### 3.3 Breakdown (`GetBreakdown`, `award_service.go:1050-1220`)

For each completed week (`< nfl_state.week`, uncapped), each roster's score is compared with every other roster's score that week. Higher scores add a win, lower ones a loss, and **ties count as neither**. Weekly rank = 1 + number of higher scores. Output per roster: `TotalWins`, `TotalLosses`, `TotalRecord`, and per week `{week, score, wins, losses, record, rank, totalTeams}`. Sorted by wins descending, then losses ascending. The roster-week score is de-duplicated (prefers non-zero, then a real `matchup_id`, then the higher score).

The playoff-seeding version (§3.4) uses a different Breakdown: **wins only**, weeks 1–14 only.

### 3.4 Playoff standings, seeding and prediction (`GetPlayoffStandings`, `:1439-2071`)

The rules source is `playoffs.md`:
- Regular season: weeks 1–14.
- **Playoffs**: 4 division winners plus 2 wildcards, seeds 1–4 by record and 5–6 by record.
- **Shit Bowl**: 4 division losers (worst first) plus the 2 worst wildcards.
- **Mediocre Bowl**: the remaining 4, seeded by record.
- Tiebreaks:
  - Within a division, 2 clubs: H2H, divisional record, Breakdown, scissors-paper-rock.
  - Within a division, 3+ clubs: divisional record, Breakdown, SPR.
  - Outside a division, 2 clubs: H2H (if applicable), Breakdown, SPR.
  - Outside a division, 3+ clubs: Breakdown, SPR.
  - Shit Bowl tiebreaks use the inverse of these.
- Bracket shape:
  - Playoffs and Shit Bowl: seeds 1 and 2 get byes; 3v6 and 4v5; then #1 meets the lowest remaining seed and #2 the highest; the final is in "round 16".
  - Mediocre Bowl: 7v10 and 8v9 in round 15, final in week 16.

How the code implements it:
- **Records** come from matchups in weeks 1–14 and `< current week`. Divisions come from `rosters[].settings.division` (live API). Team names come from Sleeper **user metadata `team_name`** (live API).
- **"Best record"** is `wins − losses`, not win % (ties are dropped).
- **Comparators are pairwise and ignore the 2-vs-3+ club distinction.**
  - Division comparator: W−L, then H2H wins, then divisional W−L, then Breakdown, then **roster_id**.
  - Overall comparator: W−L, then H2H, then Breakdown, then roster_id.
  - Because H2H is applied even in 3+-team ties, the code diverges from `playoffs.md` there. Scissors-paper-rock is replaced by the lowest roster_id.
- **Division winner / loser** = first / last after the division sort (a loser only if the division has more than 2 teams). Winners are seeded by the overall comparator. Losers are seeded by its negation (worst first).
- **Wildcards** = everyone else, sorted overall: the top 2 go to the Playoffs, the bottom 2 to the Shit Bowl (wildcard loser 1 = second-worst, 2 = worst), and the middle 4 to the Mediocre Bowl ("Seed 1–4").
- **Predictions**: the query param `predictions = {week: {matchup_id: winner_roster_id}}`, applied only for weeks `≥ current` and `≤ 14`. Each prediction adds W/L, H2H and divisional W/L but **no Breakdown**. The frontend stores predictions in `localStorage` (`PlayoffsTab.js:45-66`).
- **`CalculatePlayoffScenarios`** is a placeholder that ignores its predictions (`:2074-2090`).

**Bracket prediction (frontend only, `PlayoffBrackets.js:32-138`)** uses wildcard week 15, semis week 16 and the final week 17. The semifinal logic sorts the surviving wildcards by ascending seed and gives #1 `sorted[0]`, the **better** surviving seed. That contradicts `playoffs.md` ("#1 faces the lowest remaining seed") and build.py's re-seeding rule.

- **Privacy**: the UI labels teams by Sleeper `team_name`, or falls back to **display name / username** (`PlayoffBrackets.js:158`, `WeeklyScoresChart.js:65`).

### 3.6 Coverage

The 2025 season only (one league id, `"2025"` hardcoded in matchups, transactions and awards). It has no NFL.com-era data, no franchise concept (identity is Sleeper user / roster), and no history across seasons.

### 3.7 Known defects worth not porting

The hardcoded season, uncapped "completed weeks", the 2024 player stats and PPR mismatch, the dead One-Man Army, the Draft Dodger placeholder, user-keyed award rows (co-owner duplicates), and the semifinal pairing bug.

## 5. Overlaps and conflicts between sources

### 5.1 Duplicated computations

| Thing | ff-data-project | sleeper-awards | Conflict |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Season W-L, PF, PA (2025) | From Sleeper roster settings (regular season only) | Recomputed from matchups wk 1–14 | Should agree. sa drops ties from records. |
| Team identity | Franchise (hand alias, across eras) | Sleeper user / roster, names from Sleeper profiles | sa has no franchise concept and exposes usernames. |
| Trades / moves | `trades` (trades only, per roster), `adds` (all added players) | Pick a Lane = all completed trades, waivers and FA moves | Different definitions under similar names. |
| Brackets / finish | Reads Sleeper winners and losers brackets as truth; Shit Bowl = losers-bracket `p=1` winner | Computes seeding itself from `playoffs.md` and predicts brackets | sa's semifinal pairing contradicts both `playoffs.md` and build.py. Neither validates that Sleeper's overridden brackets match `playoffs.md`. |
| Weekly highs / lows | `bestWeek`/`worstWeek` (regular season, all logged eras) | Min/max per team (2025, all weeks) | Scope differs (phase and seasons). |
| Breakdown | Not computed | Two variants: `/breakdown` (W and L, ties ignored, all weeks before the current one) and the seeding tiebreak (wins only, wk 1–14) | GLOSSARY defines Breakdown as a record against every other franchise each week. Ties and the week range need a decision. |

### 5.2 Rule and data inconsistencies

- **Regular-season length**: `playoffs.md` and sa say weeks 1–14. The NFL API data shows 14 weeks (2012–14), **13** (2015–20) and 14 (2021–24). Sleeper 2025 has `playoff_week_start = 15`.
- **Final week**: `playoffs.md` puts the championship / Shit Bowl in "round 16", which is impossible with a 3-round bracket starting in week 15. The sa UI says week 17 for the final and week 16 for the Mediocre Bowl final. Sleeper 2025 brackets have 3 rounds (wk 15–17).
- **Format history**: `playoffs.md` describes the 16-team, 4-division, 6/6/4 format. 2012–14 had 10–12 teams, one division and 4-team championship and consolation brackets. The Mediocre Bowl can only exist from 2015.
- **Shit Bowl outcome**: ff treats the Shit Bowl as "won" by the franchise crowned worst (losers-bracket placement 1). `playoffs.md` gives seeding but doesn't say whether the bracket advances winners or losers. That needs confirming against Sleeper's `loser_bracket` settings and overrides.
- **Tiebreaks**: sa replaces scissors-paper-rock with roster_id, applies H2H in 3+-team ties, and uses W−L rather than win %.
- **Keepers**: the map's constraint that "Sleeper-era drafts and keepers are structured data" isn't met by the data. Sleeper's `is_keeper` and `keepers` are empty for 2025 and 2026. The 2025 keeper list exists only as a Google Sheet (fd).
- **README drift**: the ff README says 1,206 scraped games (the file now has 1,162 after the 2022 deletion) and "fourteen seasons, 2012–2026".

### 5.3 What only one source has

- **Only ff-data-project**: everything 2012–2024, franchise identity, `SAME_MANAGER`, the Shit Bowl list, emoji badges, the redaction, bracket reconstruction, raw Sleeper archives for 2025–26 (incl. `players_points`, transactions and drafts).
- **Only sleeper-awards**: award definitions, both Breakdown variants, `playoffs.md` (the only written seeding and tiebreak rules), the prediction UX, the Postgres schema, daily sync.
- **Only fantasy-dashboard**: links to the constitution, AGM docs 2021–2025, draft boards 2017–2024 and keeper lists 2021–2025.
